# Observations and useful measurement

Record milestone observations only when supported, never by filling guessed dates into old applications. `applications[].milestones` stores `discovered_at`, `qualified_at`, `materials_ready_at`, or `submitted_at` as `{at, source_ids}`. Discovery/qualification/materials readiness describe the time the workflow actually observed or completed that step; they are not the employer's posting date. Save the underlying source/review note first. Leave unobserved history blank.

Use `pipeline.py observe --workspace W --application APP_ID --milestone qualified_at --source-id SOURCE_ID` to record an observation now. `--observed-at` is for a known, sourced earlier timestamp with an offset. Submitted time must match the employer-confirmed `submission.confirmed_at` and use its confirmation source; the helper refuses an unverified click. Milestones are immutable; add a sourced correction note rather than overwrite a historical observation.

Keep discovery source and application channel separate in the application record (for example `discovery_source: "professional association alert"`, `application_channel: "employer ATS"`), with source evidence in the application. Do not infer one from the other or from a redirect.

Use `pipeline.py metrics --workspace W --from START --to END` with offset timestamps. The cohort includes confirmed submissions at or after START and before END. The denominator is confirmed submissions only, including those later withdrawn when a receipt remains; unverified clicks are excluded. The output reports current outcome counts/rates, unresolved outcomes, explicit cohort boundaries and the sample size. It is a current-state snapshot, not a history of every earlier stage or a prediction of future hiring probability. The “small sample” label uses a disclosed descriptive threshold of fewer than 20 confirmed submissions, not a statistical significance test.

For additional stage-conversion questions, first define the event, denominator and cohort in ordinary language and verify that the necessary observations were saved. Missing events are unknown, not zero progress. Do not invent recruiter/interview transition dates from today's outcome, compare unlike cohorts, or attribute changes to a resume revision without evidence. Review outcomes to improve queries and material selection without making causal claims from small samples.
