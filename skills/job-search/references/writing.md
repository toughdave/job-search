# Resume and cover-letter craft

Read when selecting evidence or writing employer-facing documents. Formatting, approved length, regional conventions and actual-file review stay in [documents](documents.md).

## Select before writing

Compare every central posting requirement with current evidence. Prefer a direct match under the named employer, then a relevant measured achievement, an owned responsibility with supported scale, a shipped project, education, and transferable experience. This is a selection aid, not a reason to relocate an achievement between employers or discard stronger relevant evidence just because it lacks a number. Preserve employment, project and study boundaries.

Keep a private posting-to-bullet map beside the model: requirement, selected fact IDs, employer/period and resulting bullet. Each selected bullet in the most recent relevant role should prove a central requirement or explain necessary context. Do not force every requirement into that role when evidence belongs elsewhere. Uncovered requirements stay partial/unknown rather than becoming new bullets.

## Write and edit

- Build bullets from action, system/scope, method and supported result. Lead with the useful action or user/business outcome; put tools later where they explain the work. Omit any component that is not known rather than inventing a result.
- Vary meaningful opening verbs and remove duplicate achievements. Avoid inflated verbs that imply ownership, management or sales not established by the evidence.
- A headline can use the target title alone as the desired role; it must not change historical job titles. Keep the summary factual, without superlatives or unverified totals of experience. Calculate any total from non-overlapping supported periods before using it.
- Use only supported metrics, with the original unit and scope. A rounded value must remain conservative and explain its derivation internally; do not convert tickets per week into users supported or estimate improvement percentages from anecdotes.
- Prefer month/year dates where known. Preserve year-only precision when that is all the candidate supplied; exact-day requirements belong to forms. Do not invent months for consistent-looking formatting.
- Before shrinking type or spacing, drop low-value tools, repeated claims and irrelevant older detail. Respect the approved page standard and readability bounds; useful white space is better than fabricated filler.

Use a concise, natural voice and ordinary punctuation. Avoid habitual em dashes, repetitive sentence patterns, flattery and corporate boilerplate. Keep gaps in the internal fit map. Do not volunteer “I do not have X” in a resume or letter; answer a direct screening question truthfully and discuss a material gap only when it helps explain relevant transferable evidence.

## Cover letter

When the portal accepts a letter, prepare a tailored one unless the candidate opts out or the employer prohibits it. Check optional, additional/supporting-document and text-entry routes. Use the actual employer and role, with no stale names from another application.

Use a brief opening and two or three short evidence paragraphs connecting the candidate's strongest relevant work to this role. Mention at most one material gap when useful; do not turn the letter into a list of deficiencies. Omit salary unless specifically requested, generic praise and desperate appeals. Preserve factual source links internally and use the same clean employer/role filename stem as the resume.

Before final review, either verify the actual letter attachment/text or record the candidate's opt-out, employer prohibition, genuinely absent route, or precise failed control. If an available control fails, inspect fresh page state and try one supported alternative interaction. If that also fails, disclose the exact problem before submission. A broken optional control is not an absent route. Complete visual review before upload and verify the uploaded letter at the portal's final review.

## Optional reusable role-family packets

Offer these only after repeated tailoring makes them useful. Store candidate-approved factual blocks, fact IDs, occupation/destination/length scope, approval source, source revisions and template hashes in this private project. Use them to accelerate selection, not freeze wording. Before reuse, recheck corrected/superseded evidence, the current posting and template hashes. Invalidate affected blocks when evidence changes; never reuse another person's packet or treat approval of a block as submission approval.
